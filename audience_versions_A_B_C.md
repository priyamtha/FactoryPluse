## 2. Task 6: Audience-Adapted Executive Summaries

### Version A: For Board of Directors (Strategic Risk & Shareholder Value)
Customer churn currently erodes $2M in annual recurring revenue (7% churn rate vs 4% industry benchmark), creating a significant strategic growth headwind and depressing valuation multiples. Our data audit reveals that operational support delays account for 40% of this revenue leak. By authorizing a targeted $250K investment ($200K support headcount, $50K priority routing engineering), we project a $400K annual churn reduction, yielding a 2x Year 1 ROI, protecting high-value enterprise relationships, and strengthening shareholder value.

---

### Version B: For Operations Team (Implementation Details & Timeline)
To reduce customer churn from 7% to 3%, we are launching an operational overhaul focused on cutting support first-response times from 6 hours to under 2 hours. Operations will partner with HR to post job descriptions for 2 new Support Engineers by Dec 1, complete interviewing by Jan 31, and finish onboarding by Apr 1. Concurrently, Operations will document a formal <2 hour response time SLA and deploy daily dashboard tracking by Jan 1.

Furthermore, Operations will collaborate with Engineering to implement automated priority ticket routing for accounts spending >$10K/year, launching live by Feb 1. These process improvements will systematically reduce ticket backlogs, enforce operational accountability, and protect high-value revenue streams.

---

### Version C: For Support Team (Work Environment & Burnout Prevention)
We hear you: support ticket volumes have grown 40% year-over-year, creating heavy shift workloads and pushing response times to 6 hours despite your best efforts. To eliminate ticket backlog stress and prevent team burnout, leadership has approved hiring 2 additional full-time Support Engineers to expand shift coverage and lighten individual workloads.

These new headcount additions will give our team the breathing room needed to deliver fast, high-quality help (<2 hour target) without feeling overwhelmed. In addition, an automated priority queue will automatically route complex enterprise tickets to dedicated specialists, allowing you to focus on resolving customer issues effectively while improving your daily work environment.
