# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE
## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is our leading revenue loss driver, costing $2M annually. Our current churn rate stands at 7% vs the industry average of 4%. We analyzed root causes across 50,000 accounts and identified a high-impact operational opportunity to reverse revenue loss.

### Key Findings
- **Finding 1:** Support speed directly impacts retention. Customers receiving support within 2 hours churn at 3%. Customers waiting >24 hours churn at 12% (4x risk difference).
- **Finding 2:** Our current support team averages a 6-hour response time. This delay is our primary customer retention bottleneck.
- **Finding 3:** High-value customers (>$10K annual spend) are particularly sensitive to support delays. They churn at 15% when support is slow.

### Business Risks
- **Revenue at Risk:** At our current 7% churn rate, we lose $2M annually. The high-value customer segment is especially vulnerable.
- **Competitive Vulnerability:** Competitors with faster support are actively recruiting our dissatisfied customers.
- **Operational Burnout & Degradation:** Support ticket volume grew 40% year-over-year while response times degraded, threatening team burnout and worsening churn.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (Cost: $200K/year)
   - Reduces average response time from 6 hours to <2 hours target.
   - Expected impact: Recover $400K in annual revenue through churn reduction (2x ROI in Year 1).

2. **Implement Response Time SLA** (Cost: $0)
   - Document <2 hour first-response target and track daily.
   - Creates team accountability and operational prioritization by Jan 1.

3. **Prioritize High-Value Customers** (Cost: $50K engineering)
   - Route $10K+/year accounts to a dedicated priority support queue.
   - Expected impact: Reduce high-value customer churn by 50% by Feb 1.

### Decision Needed
Approve hiring budget ($200K) and engineering resources ($50K) by Dec 15. Projected ROI: $400K churn reduction in Year 1 (Net gain: $150K).
